# Welcome email — send to every new signup, by hand, for the first ~50

**Why by hand:** the automated welcome email cannot send right now (no email provider is
configured on production — see the Day-1 checklist). Even once it can, a personal note
from the founder in the first weeks converts far better than a template.

**Send within a few hours of the signup alert.** Plain text, from your own address.

---

**Subject:** Thanks for trying RestoreAssist — one thing to do first

Hi {FIRST NAME},

Phill here — I built RestoreAssist. Thanks for signing up.

One thing before you start, because it trips everyone up: RestoreAssist runs on your own
AI key, not mine. That's deliberate — it means you pay the AI provider directly at cost
(usually a few dollars a month) instead of me marking it up and charging you for it.

So the first step is:

  Settings → AI Providers → paste an Anthropic or OpenAI API key

It takes about two minutes. Until that's in, report generation won't run. After it's in,
everything works.

Once you're set up, the fastest way to see whether this is for you:

  1. Create a job with a real address
  2. Add a few moisture readings
  3. Generate the report

That's the whole loop. If the report isn't something you'd be happy sending to an
insurer, I want to know why — that's the most useful thing you could tell me.

Your trial runs 15 days and includes 50 report credits, so there's plenty of room to
try it on real work.

If you'd rather I just walked you through it, reply and I'll call you. I'm in
{YOUR TIMEZONE} and I answer my own email.

Phill
RestoreAssist
restoreassist.app
