# RestoreAssist in 10 minutes — first job to first report

Attach this to the welcome email, or paste it inline.

## Before anything else: add your AI key (2 min)

RestoreAssist runs on **your** AI key. You pay Anthropic or OpenAI directly, at cost —
typically a few dollars a month for normal use. Nothing else works until this is done.

1. Get a key from **console.anthropic.com** (or **platform.openai.com**)
2. In RestoreAssist: **Settings → AI Providers**
3. Paste it, save

You'll know it worked when the onboarding checklist stops asking for it.

## 1. Create the job (2 min)

**Inspections → New.** The only required fields are the property address and postcode.
Everything else can be filled in as you go — you're not filling in a form, you're
building a record while you work.

## 2. Capture what you saw (4 min)

- **Moisture readings** — location, material, reading. The app flags each one against the
  IICRC dry standard for that material, so you see dry / drying / wet as you enter them.
- **Photos** — attach them to the job; they land in the report automatically.
- **Environmental** — ambient temp and RH. Dew point is calculated for you.
- **Affected areas** — the rooms and what's damaged in each.

You do not need all of it for a first report. Address + a few readings is enough to see
what the output looks like.

## 3. Generate the report (2 min)

**Reports → New**, pick the job, generate. You get an IICRC S500:2021-referenced report
with your readings, photos and scope in it.

That's one of your 50 trial credits.

## 4. Check it reads right

Open the PDF. This is the part worth your attention — is this something you'd send to an
insurer without editing? If not, tell us exactly where it falls short. That feedback is
worth more to us than a subscription right now.

## What it costs after the trial

- **50 report credits** included each month
- Extra reports **$1.98** each
