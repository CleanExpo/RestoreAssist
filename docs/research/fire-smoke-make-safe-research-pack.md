# Research pack: fire and smoke make-safe and initial inspection (Victoria)

Prepared 11/11/2026 for the RestoreAssist Voice Copilot fire and smoke walkthrough.
Test scenario (fictional): a house in metropolitan Melbourne, deliberately lit twice, ground floor gutted, insurer-funded claim, about a month since the fire, no make-safe done yet. No real client details appear here.

Review status: UNPROVEN. Written by a research agent on 11/11/2026. Not yet independently reviewed or passed through the TRD gate. The app's checklist (lib/voice/fire-smoke/checklist.ts) cites the VERIFIED rows below by ID; no UNVERIFIED row is cited.

Rules this pack follows:
- Primary sources only. Every source was opened on 11/11/2026 unless marked UNVERIFIED.
- No IICRC or Standards Australia body text was searched for, opened, quoted or paraphrased. Those rows carry publisher landing-page metadata only (title, edition, year, AS adoption, one-line public scope).
- Victoria does not use the model WHS Act. Every step names the Victorian instrument first and the Safe Work Australia (SWA) model equivalent second.
- Section and regulation numbers below come from the official Word versions of the Acts and Regulations, downloaded from legislation.vic.gov.au and safeworkaustralia.gov.au and read in full text. A number not in those files is not given.

Confidence key: H = provision read directly in the in-force text; M = official guidance page read, or provision found from the table of contents only; L = inferred, or source not opened.
