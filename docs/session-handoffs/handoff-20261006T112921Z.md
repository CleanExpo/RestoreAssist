## 1. Summary of what was done

Senior PM Walkthrough round 6 resumed at its unchecked blocker 4 — a CI guard
against pricing drift. Writing the guard found drift that was already live: the
marketing home claimed every add-on was $11/month while `FLOORPLAN_UNDERLAY` was
$9.95, and the pricing page said "from $9.95".

The owner's instruction was "just make everything $11". That shipped, along with
the guard, and with five buyer-facing surfaces changed from typed-in prices to
values derived from the SSOT. One of those five was an in-app upgrade CTA that
would have gone on quoting $9.95 while Stripe charged $11.

Separately, a question from the owner — "Are you checking Digital Ocean also?" —
exposed that I had been treating Vercel as production. It is not. Production is
the DigitalOcean app in `.do/app.yaml`, and the launch checklist said otherwise.

## 2. Where it started

Carried in: round 6 was paused two findings deep, with blocker 4 never checked.

## 3. Decisions locked + what shipped

**Merged — #2403, squashed to `062c2e4` on `main`.**

- **Every recurring add-on is $11/month. The $9.95 was never sanctioned.** I
  first wrote a comment claiming `docs/specs/byok-monetisation-spec.md` §2 named
  the $9.95 as an exception. Reading §2 showed it says "five $11/month add-ons"
  and never mentions $9.95. So this change *restores* the documented model rather
  than departing from it. The comment was rewritten.
- **Levelling up, not qualifying the copy.** The alternative was to change the
  marketing claim to "from $9.95". The owner chose the price.
- **Legacy subscribers keep paying $9.95, deliberately and indefinitely.**
  Stripe prices inline via `price_data` at checkout, so existing add-on
  subscriptions are untouched. There is no proration or migration in this path.
  Expect a legacy cohort alongside new $11 subscriptions. Recorded in the
  module's doc comment, because revenue reconciliation will otherwise read it as
  a bug.
- **The guard asserts the copy's *shape*, not a number.** When every add-on is
  the same price the home copy must say "add-ons are $X"; when they differ it
  must say "from $X". Levelling the prices without fixing the copy fails it, and
  so does the reverse.
- **The advisory was fixed via the repo's own `overrides` idiom, not suppressed.**
  `source-map-js: ">=1.2.2"` added to `package.json` `overrides`. It was
  deliberately **not** added to `auditConfig.ignoreGhsas`.
